# Verification and interpretation notes

Original reviewer reports are frozen and are not silently corrected here. This record distinguishes original-source checks from experimental replication. No experiments were replicated.

## Load-bearing checks

## Consultation limits

The two prior-knowledge reports used the same neutral problem and requested Bitter Lesson skill. Their convergence is useful design judgment, not independent empirical evidence. The skill itself can encourage skepticism of fixed methods. Neither report saw the existing package or its proposed replacement, so neither is a blind A/B judgment of those exact artifacts.

The lead and Astra literature reviews overlap on several sources. Count each original study once. Different models finding the same paper does not multiply its sample size or remove its methodological limitations. The lead is not blind to the earlier discussion. Opus continues Fable's research in one isolated session, so those two models do not supply two independent literature reviews.

The verification here prioritizes claims used in the final recommendation. It does not certify every ancillary citation in every initial report, all public commentary, or all underlying datasets and code. Raw reviewer reports remain attributable opinions. The final recommendation must preserve positive, negative and null evidence and must not be described as a measured improvement of the user's guidance.
