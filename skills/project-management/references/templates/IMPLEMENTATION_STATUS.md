## Status vocabulary

- `not_started` — planned, no durable evidence yet
- `in_progress` — active work, exit evidence incomplete
- `blocked` — needs decision, dependency, or artifact
- `done` — complete with evidence in repo
- `deferred` — intentionally postponed
- `cancelled` — removed from scope
