# SF1 artifact delivery

For authorized Snowflake1 work that produces a browser app, review, or visual research artifact the user needs to open remotely, check for `~/.config/snowflake1/artifacts-box.json`. Its presence is the host's enrollment signal. Read the usage instructions it links to; the project-owned `artifacts-box` skill supports private delivery and recovery when installed.

Keep the default about the outcome: a usable private link and preserved source/data. Choose the appropriate format and tools. Do not upload unrelated personal material, override local-only or retired-app decisions, or infer publication authority from the configuration. Using the service does not connect the source repository to Snowflake1. If the service is not ready, disclose the limitation and retain the local result; do not claim remote delivery.
